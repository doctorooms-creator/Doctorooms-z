    11→    11→    11→- **Location**: `application/core/MY_Controller.php` (line 605-632)

    18→    18→    18→  - `_alertSuccessResponce($success, $success_msg, $fail_msg, $re_url)` — sets flash feedback, redirects

    20→    20→    20→  - `_alertWarningResponce(...)` — same but with 'alert' class

    23→    23→    23→  - `_generateSeoURL($string)` — URL slug generator
    24→    24→    24→  - `_generate_thumb(...)` — creates 300x300 thumbnail
    25→    25→    25→  - `send_mail(...)` — sends email via CI email library
    26→    26→    26→  - `sendMobileMessage(...)` — sends SMS via curl to soft-tech-solutions.com
