     7→## 📌 WHY NURSE IS THE MOST CRITICAL ROLE
     8→
     9→Bhai, doctor ki nazar se samjho:
    10→
    11→- **Doctor** hospital mein 10-15 minute aata hai round pe — patient dekhta hai, notes likhta hai, order deta hai, chala jaata hai
    12→- **Nurse** 24/7 rehti hai — **WOHI HAI JO ACTUALLY ILLAJ KARTI HAI**
    13→- Doctor ne likha "Inj. Enoxaparin 60mg SC BD" — par **inject kaun dega?** → Nurse
    14→- Doctor ne likha "Monitor BP hourly" — par **BP kaun measure karega?** → Nurse
    15→- Patient raat 3 baje chillane laga — **kaun pehle pohochega?** → Nurse
    16→- Patient ka urine output kam ho gaya — **kaun notice karega?** → Nurse (monitoring chart dekh ke)
    17→
    18→**Nurse is the eyes and hands of the doctor 24/7.** Ye role bilkul alag level ka hai.

    22→## 🏗️ NURSE ROLE IN HOSPITAL STRUCTURE
    23→
    24→```
    25→Hospital
    26→  ├── Ward 1: General Ward (10 beds)
    27→  │     ├── Nurse A (Morning 7AM-2PM) → Patients: Bed 1,2,3,4,5
    28→  │     ├── Nurse B (Morning 7AM-2PM) → Patients: Bed 6,7,8,9,10
    29→  │     ├── Nurse C (Evening 2PM-9PM)  → Patients: Bed 1,2,3,4,5
    30→  │     └── Nurse D (Evening 2PM-9PM)  → Patients: Bed 6,7,8,9,10
    31→  │
    32→  ├── Ward 2: Private Ward (6 beds)
    33→  │     ├── Nurse E (Morning) → Patients: Bed 1,2,3
    34→  │     └── Nurse F (Morning) → Patients: Bed 4,5,6
    35→  │
    36→  ├── Ward 3: ICU (4 beds)
    37→  │     ├── Nurse G (Morning) → Patient: Bed 1 ONLY (ICU = 1:1 or 1:2 ratio)
    38→  │     ├── Nurse H (Morning) → Patient: Bed 2,3
    39→  │     └── Nurse I (Morning) → Patient: Bed 4
    40→  │
    41→  └── Nursing Incharge (Head Nurse — manages all wards, assigns nurses)
    42→```
    43→
    44→### Key Concepts:
    45→1. **Nurse-to-Patient Ratio:** ICU mein 1 nurse per 1-2 patient. General ward mein 1 nurse per 5-8 patients
    46→2. **Shift System:** 3 shifts — Morning (7AM-2PM), Evening (2PM-9PM), Night (9PM-7AM)
    47→3. **Shift Handover:** Jab nurse ki shift khatam hoti hai, usko next shift nurse ko POORA update dena padta hai
    48→4. **Ward-based Assignment:** Nurse ek specific ward mein assigned hoti hai, us ward ke hi patients sambhalti hai
    49→5. **Nursing Incharge:** Head nurse jo sab nurses manage karti hai, bed allocation karti hai

    55→### Scenario: Raju (Heart Attack) admitted in ICU Bed 3

    63→    Next Enoxaparin due at 8AM."
    64→  ↓
    65→② VIEW MY PATIENTS LIST
    66→   Bed 1: Suresh (Post-Op Appendectomy) — Stable
    67→   Bed 2: Meena (Pneumonia) — On Oxygen 4L
    68→   Bed 3: Raju (Heart Attack) — CRITICAL ⚠️
    69→  ↓
    70→③ CHECK PENDING MEDICINES (8:00 AM)
    71→   Raju ke liye due:
    72→   ✅ Tab. Metformin 500mg — Oral — Given at 8:05 AM
    73→   ✅ Inj. Enoxaparin 60mg — SC — Given at 8:10 AM
    74→   ✅ Tab. Aspirin 75mg — Oral — Given at 8:05 AM
    75→   ❌ Inj. Pantoprazole 40mg — IV — Pharmacy se medicine nahi aayi → Notified
    76→  ↓
    77→④ RECORD VITALS (8:00 AM — Every Hour)
    78→   Raju:
    79→   Temp: 98.4°F | Pulse: 82 | SpO2: 97% | BP: 112/74 | RR: 18
    80→   Input: 250ml (IV NS) | Urine: 120ml | Remarks: "Pain reduced, comfortable"
    81→  ↓
    82→⑤ SAMPLE COLLECTION (9:00 AM)
    83→   Doctor ne order kiya: CBC, KFT, LFT, RBS
    84→   Nurse Priya sample collect karti hai → Lab bhejti hai
    85→   ✅ Blood sample collected at 9:15 AM, sent to lab
    86→  ↓
    87→⑥ RECORD VITALS (9:00 AM)
    88→   Temp: 98.6°F | Pulse: 80 | SpO2: 98% | BP: 118/76 | RR: 18
    89→   Input: 200ml (Oral water) | Urine: 100ml | Remarks: "Stable"
    90→  ↓
    91→⑦ DOCTOR ROUNDS (10:00 AM)
    92→   Dr. Patel aate hain:
    93→   Nurse Priya presents: "Sir, Raju — BP stable 112-118/74-76, 
    94→   pain significantly reduced, urine output adequate 100-120ml/hr.
    95→   Blood sample sent at 9:15, reports pending."
    96→   Dr. Patel: "Good. Continue same. Stop ECG monitoring if stable by evening. 
    97→   Add Tab. Atorvastatin 40mg HS."
    98→   Nurse Priya updates order sheet with new medicine
    99→  ↓
   100→⑧ HOURLY VITALS CONTINUE (10AM, 11AM, 12PM, 1PM...)
   101→   Every hour: Temp, Pulse, BP, SpO2, RR, Input, Urine, Remarks
   102→  ↓
   103→⑨ PENDING MEDICINES (2:00 PM)
   104→   Inj. Enoxaparin 60mg — SC — Given at 2:00 PM
   105→   Tab. Metformin 500mg — Oral — Given at 2:05 PM
   106→  ↓
   107→⑩ SHIFT HANDOVER (2:00 PM — Before leaving)
   108→   Nurse Priya writes:
   109→   "Bed 3 Raju — Stable since morning. BP range 110-120/72-78.
   110→   Pain free. Urine output adequate. Reports: CBC normal, 
   111→   KFT pending, LFT pending. New order: Atorvastatin 40mg HS (not yet given, 
   112→   due tonight). Doctor satisfied with progress."
   113→   ↓
   114→   Nurse Sunita (Evening shift) reads this and takes over
   115→```

   637→## 🔄 NURSE'S INTERACTION WITH OTHER ROLES
   638→
   639→### Nurse ↔ Doctor:
   640→
   641→| Scenario | Flow |
   642→|----------|------|
   643→| **Doctor writes order** | Doctor → DoctorOrder table → Nurse sees pending medicines → Nurse administers → MedicineAdministration table |
   644→| **Nurse reports abnormal vitals** | Nurse records vital → System auto-checks thresholds → Alert sent to Doctor → Doctor reviews → May write new order |
   645→| **Doctor comes for round** | Doctor sees VitalRecord + MedicineAdministration history → Doctor writes DoctorVisit notes → May add/stop DoctorOrders |
   646→| **Doctor orders investigation** | Doctor → DoctorOrder (type=investigation) OR separate investigation order → Nurse sees → Nurse collects sample → SampleCollection table |
   647→
   648→### Nurse ↔ Pharmacist:
   649→
   650→| Scenario | Flow |
   651→|----------|------|
   652→| **Medicine not available** | Nurse tries to administer → Marks "NotAvailable" → Pharmacist sees alert → Pharmacy arranges medicine → Nurse notified |
   653→| **New medicine needed** | Doctor writes new order → Pharmacist sees order → Prepares/dispenses to ward → Nurse receives → Administers |
   654→
   655→### Nurse ↔ Receptionist:
   656→
   657→| Scenario | Flow |
   658→|----------|------|
   659→| **New patient admitted** | Receptionist creates IpdAdmission → Bed allocated → Nurse assigned → Nurse sees new patient in "My Patients" |
   660→| **Patient discharge** | Doctor says discharge → Nurse does final vitals, removes IV lines → Receptionist processes billing → Bed freed |
   661→
   662→### Nurse ↔ Lab:
   663→
   664→| Scenario | Flow |
   665→|----------|------|
   666→| **Sample collection** | Doctor orders investigation → Nurse collects sample → Sends to lab → Lab enters results → InvestigationReport → Doctor reviews |
   667→
   668→### Nurse ↔ Nursing Incharge (Head Nurse):
   669→
   670→| Scenario | Flow |
   671→|----------|------|
   672→| **Shift assignment** | Nursing Incharge assigns nurses to patients for each shift → NursePatientAssignment |
   673→| **Re-assignment** | If nurse sick/emergency → Incharge re-assigns patients to another nurse |
   674→| **Ward management** | Incharge manages bed availability, nurse schedules, leave management |
