    22→    22→## 🏗️ NURSE ROLE IN HOSPITAL STRUCTURE
    23→    23→
    24→    24→```
    25→    25→Hospital
    26→    26→  ├── Ward 1: General Ward (10 beds)
    27→    27→  │     ├── Nurse A (Morning 7AM-2PM) → Patients: Bed 1,2,3,4,5
    28→    28→  │     ├── Nurse B (Morning 7AM-2PM) → Patients: Bed 6,7,8,9,10
    29→    29→  │     ├── Nurse C (Evening 2PM-9PM)  → Patients: Bed 1,2,3,4,5
    30→    30→  │     └── Nurse D (Evening 2PM-9PM)  → Patients: Bed 6,7,8,9,10
    31→    31→  │
    32→    32→  ├── Ward 2: Private Ward (6 beds)
    33→    33→  │     ├── Nurse E (Morning) → Patients: Bed 1,2,3
    34→    34→  │     └── Nurse F (Morning) → Patients: Bed 4,5,6
    35→    35→  │
    36→    36→  ├── Ward 3: ICU (4 beds)
    37→    37→  │     ├── Nurse G (Morning) → Patient: Bed 1 ONLY (ICU = 1:1 or 1:2 ratio)
    38→    38→  │     ├── Nurse H (Morning) → Patient: Bed 2,3
    39→    39→  │     └── Nurse I (Morning) → Patient: Bed 4
    40→    40→  │
    41→    41→  └── Nursing Incharge (Head Nurse — manages all wards, assigns nurses)
    42→    42→```
    43→    43→
    44→    44→### Key Concepts:
    45→    45→1. **Nurse-to-Patient Ratio:** ICU mein 1 nurse per 1-2 patient. General ward mein 1 nurse per 5-8 patients
    46→    46→2. **Shift System:** 3 shifts — Morning (7AM-2PM), Evening (2PM-9PM), Night (9PM-7AM)
    47→    47→3. **Shift Handover:** Jab nurse ki shift khatam hoti hai, usko next shift nurse ko POORA update dena padta hai
    48→    48→4. **Ward-based Assignment:** Nurse ek specific ward mein assigned hoti hai, us ward ke hi patients sambhalti hai
    49→    49→5. **Nursing Incharge:** Head nurse jo sab nurses manage karti hai, bed allocation karti hai

   637→   637→## 🔄 NURSE'S INTERACTION WITH OTHER ROLES
   638→   638→
   639→   639→### Nurse ↔ Doctor:
   640→   640→
   641→   641→| Scenario | Flow |
   642→   642→|----------|------|
   643→   643→| **Doctor writes order** | Doctor → DoctorOrder table → Nurse sees pending medicines → Nurse administers → MedicineAdministration table |
   644→   644→| **Nurse reports abnormal vitals** | Nurse records vital → System auto-checks thresholds → Alert sent to Doctor → Doctor reviews → May write new order |
   645→   645→| **Doctor comes for round** | Doctor sees VitalRecord + MedicineAdministration history → Doctor writes DoctorVisit notes → May add/stop DoctorOrders |
   646→   646→| **Doctor orders investigation** | Doctor → DoctorOrder (type=investigation) OR separate investigation order → Nurse sees → Nurse collects sample → SampleCollection table |
   647→   647→
   648→   648→### Nurse ↔ Pharmacist:
   649→   649→
   650→   650→| Scenario | Flow |
   651→   651→|----------|------|
   652→   652→| **Medicine not available** | Nurse tries to administer → Marks "NotAvailable" → Pharmacist sees alert → Pharmacy arranges medicine → Nurse notified |
   653→   653→| **New medicine needed** | Doctor writes new order → Pharmacist sees order → Prepares/dispenses to ward → Nurse receives → Administers |
   654→   654→
   655→   655→### Nurse ↔ Receptionist:
   656→   656→
   657→   657→| Scenario | Flow |
   658→   658→|----------|------|
   659→   659→| **New patient admitted** | Receptionist creates IpdAdmission → Bed allocated → Nurse assigned → Nurse sees new patient in "My Patients" |
   660→   660→| **Patient discharge** | Doctor says discharge → Nurse does final vitals, removes IV lines → Receptionist processes billing → Bed freed |
   661→   661→
   662→   662→### Nurse ↔ Lab:
   663→   663→
   664→   664→| Scenario | Flow |
   665→   665→|----------|------|
   666→   666→| **Sample collection** | Doctor orders investigation → Nurse collects sample → Sends to lab → Lab enters results → InvestigationReport → Doctor reviews |
   667→   667→
   668→   668→### Nurse ↔ Nursing Incharge (Head Nurse):
   669→   669→
   670→   670→| Scenario | Flow |
   671→   671→|----------|------|
   672→   672→| **Shift assignment** | Nursing Incharge assigns nurses to patients for each shift → NursePatientAssignment |
   673→   673→| **Re-assignment** | If nurse sick/emergency → Incharge re-assigns patients to another nurse |
   674→   674→| **Ward management** | Incharge manages bed availability, nurse schedules, leave management |

   939→   939→## 🔑 KEY ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS
   940→   940→
   941→   941→### Decision 1: IPD Admission = Separate from OPD Booking
   942→   942→
   943→   943→```
   944→   944→OPD Booking (existing Booking model)
   945→   945→  → Patient comes, sees doctor, goes home
   946→   946→  → Token system, queue, prescription
   947→   947→
   948→   948→IPD Admission (new IpdAdmission model)
   949→   949→  → Patient stays in hospital on a bed
   950→   950→  → No tokens, no queue
   951→   951→  → Continuous monitoring, orders, vitals
   952→   952→  → Separate billing, separate flow
   953→   953→```
   954→   954→
   955→   955→**Why separate?** Because IPD is fundamentally different from OPD:
   956→   956→- OPD = transactional (one visit, done)
   957→   957→- IPD = continuous (days/weeks of care)
   958→   958→- OPD has one doctor visit → IPD has daily doctor visits + 24/7 nurse care
   959→   959→- OPD prescription = one time → IPD orders = continuous, can be added/stopped/modified
   960→   960→- OPD has no vitals → IPD has hourly vitals
   961→   961→
   962→   962→**Link:** A patient CAN be admitted from OPD. If doctor says "admit karo" during OPD visit, then:
   963→   963→- OPD Booking exists (the visit)
   964→   964→- IpdAdmission created with `opdBookingId` linking to that Booking
   965→   965→- This link helps: patient demographics auto-fill from OPD data
   966→   966→
   967→   967→### Decision 2: Nurse-Patient Assignment is Shift-Based
   968→   968→
   969→   969→```
   970→   970→Shift 1 (Morning 7AM-2PM):
   971→   971→  Nurse Priya → [Bed 1, Bed 2, Bed 3]
   972→   972→  Nurse Sunita → [Bed 4, Bed 5, Bed 6]
   973→   973→
   974→   974→Shift 2 (Evening 2PM-9PM):
   975→   975→  Nurse Anita → [Bed 1, Bed 2, Bed 3]  ← Different nurse!
   976→   976→  Nurse Priya → [Bed 4, Bed 5, Bed 6]  ← Can swap
   977→   977→```
   978→   978→
   979→   979→**Why shift-based?** Because in real hospitals, different nurses work different shifts. Patient ko SAME NURSE 24/7 nahi milti. Har shift change pe naya nurse aati hai, handover hota hai.
   980→   980→
   981→   981→**Assignment logic:**
   982→   982→- Nursing Incharge (or Admin) assigns nurses to shifts
   983→   983→- At shift start, system shows "My Patients for this shift"
   984→   984→- At shift end, nurse MUST complete handover before leaving
   985→   985→
   986→   986→### Decision 3: Doctor Orders are NOT one-time prescriptions
   987→   987→
   988→   988→```
   989→   989→OPD Prescription (existing PMedicine model):
   990→   990→  → Tab. Paracetamol 500mg: Morning 1, Evening 1, Days 5
   991→   991→  → Fixed duration, one-time prescription
   992→   992→
   993→   993→IPD Doctor Order (new DoctorOrder model):
   994→   994→  → Inj. Enoxaparin 60mg SC BD
   995→   995→  → Start: Today | End: NULL (runs until doctor stops it)
   996→   996→  → Every instance = one MedicineAdministration record
   997→   997→  → Doctor can STOP the order anytime
   998→   998→  → Nurse marks each instance as Given/Missed/Refused
   999→   999→```
  1000→  1000→
  1001→  1001→**Why?** IPD mein dawaiyaan continuously chalti hain. Doctor ne likh diya, nurse Roz Roz de rahi hai, jab tak doctor na bole "band karo". Ye OPD prescription se fundamentally alag hai.
  1002→  1002→
  1003→  1003→### Decision 4: Vitals are Time-Series Data
  1004→  1004→
  1005→  1005→```
  1006→  1006→VitalRecord is NOT a snapshot — it's a TIME SERIES:
  1007→  1007→  8:00 AM → Temp 98.4, Pulse 82, BP 112/74
  1008→  1008→  9:00 AM → Temp 98.6, Pulse 80, BP 118/76
  1009→  1009→  10:00 AM → Temp 98.4, Pulse 78, BP 120/78
  1010→  1010→  ...every hour...
  1011→  1011→```
  1012→  1012→
  1013→  1013→**Why?** Because trends matter more than single readings:
  1014→  1014→- BP 120/80 once = normal
  1015→  1015→- BP 120/80 → 115/75 → 108/70 → 95/60 over 4 hours = **SHOCK** (trend is falling!)
  1016→  1016→- Doctor needs to see the TREND, not just the latest value
